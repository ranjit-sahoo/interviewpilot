About MockRep

A free AI interview coach, built by one person.

Hi, I am Ranjit Kumar Sahoo. I have worked in US IT staffing since 2017. In that time I have seen many capable people lose offers because they were not ready for the interview itself: answers that wandered, no clear example, or a resume that did not match the job.

MockRep is my attempt to give everyone the practice round that good coaching gives. You paste your resume and the job description, and you get a prep pack, a mock interview with scored feedback, and a report you can act on.

What it does

  • Builds a prep pack from your resume and the job description.
  • Runs a mock interview, by typing or by voice, and scores each answer.
  • Reviews your resume against the job and shows how to rewrite weak lines.
  • Gives you a report with pace, filler words and a stronger sample answer, which you can download as a PDF.

What it does not do

  • It does not promise a job or a score. AI feedback can be wrong, so check what matters.
  • It does not sell your data, show ads or use trackers. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
  • It does not show made-up reviews. The sample report on the home page is real output from the app on a fictional resume.

Free today, and later

MockRep is free to use. If paid plans ever launch, the price and a clear refund policy will be published on this site before anyone is asked to pay, and the Terms will be updated first.
