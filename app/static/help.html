For job seekers

Is MockRep free?

Yes. Everything is free to use, with no payment needed.

Do I need an account?

No. You can practise as a guest. An account only saves your history. Choose "Job seeker" when you sign up.

My voice is not detected.

Use Chrome on Android or a computer, allow the microphone when asked, speak close to the phone, and avoid noise. Speech recognition is done by your browser and can miss words depending on accent and the browser. In a screening, we do not judge your accent or pronunciation.

Is my audio recorded?

No. Your browser turns speech into text. We never receive or store audio.

I got a screening link from a recruiter. What happens?

You see a consent screen, answer a few questions by voice, and may take short tests. You can take it only once. Your answers are shown to the recruiter and deleted automatically after the period shown on the consent screen. You can also delete them yourself on the last screen.

Can I use the same account as a recruiter?

No. Job seeker and recruiter accounts are separate and each has its own tools. Use a different email for each.

For recruiters

What do the scores mean?

Scores are a first-round guide made with simple rules (keywords, answer length and structure, typing accuracy, quiz results). They are not a final decision and not a measure of someone's ability or character.

What are "signals"?

Soft hints such as very similar answers between candidates, leaving the page, or unusually fast answers. They are not proof of cheating. Talk to the candidate before deciding. We cannot see other apps on their phone and we do not check identity.

How do I invite many candidates?

Create a campaign, paste names and phone numbers, and send each link on WhatsApp. Or share the open link once.

How do I add colleagues?

In Company & Team, share the recruiter code (full access) or viewer code (read only). Colleagues use it when they sign up.

Can I get my data out?

Yes: CSV and print from each screen, or the API from Company & Team.
