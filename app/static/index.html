RResult: the outcome, with a number if you can

What AI does MockRep use?

NVIDIA Nemotron models, served by Nebius Token Factory. Your text is sent there to produce your result. Our account has Zero Data Retention turned on, so Nebius is set not to keep prompts or outputs.

Do you store my resume?

Guest sessions are kept for up to 2 days so you can continue, then removed. If you make an account, only the items you save and your scores are kept. You can delete your account and data any time. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Is my voice recorded?

Speech is turned into text by your own browser. Audio is never sent to or stored on our server. The playback in your report plays from your own device and disappears when you close the page.

Does it train on my data?

No. There are no ads and no trackers.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is a daily limit on AI use so the free service stays available. If the limit is reached, the question bank, coding practice and resume builder still work.
